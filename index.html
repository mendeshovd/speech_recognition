    Settings and privacy

    Used for call audio. It downloads once, then Chrome keeps it, and the call audio never leaves your computer. Whisper sometimes smooths over small grammar mistakes when it writes the text, so listen for errors it may have fixed.

    Without a key you still get grammar checks from LanguageTool. With the microphone, Chrome sends the audio to Google's speech service. Each sentence's text goes to LanguageTool, and to Anthropic when a key is set. Keep that in mind with student data.
